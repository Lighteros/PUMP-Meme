Know your token.
Know your rewards.

What exactly am I holding?

You hold $PUMP ("Pretty Unreal Massive Pair"), a community token on Solana. Always verify the full contract address before trading.

How does holding turn into BTC?

Trading on the $PUMP / BTC pair creates fees. A portion of those fees is distributed to eligible holders as BTC, sent straight to their wallets.

Are rewards guaranteed?

No. Rewards depend on trading activity and are variable. Nothing here is a promise of returns.

Do I need to connect my wallet or claim here?

No. This site never asks you to connect a wallet, sign anything or claim anything. Payouts arrive on their own.

Where is the contract address?

It hasn't been published yet. When it is, it will appear in the strip at the top of this page and on @PUMPMassivePair. Ignore any other address.
